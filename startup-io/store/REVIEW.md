## App Review instructions

1. Open the settings icon, select **EN**, then **Close**.
2. Tap **Enter the arena**, then **Let’s go**.
3. Drag anywhere to steer. BOOST adds speed. After movement begins, PIVOT grants
   a short escape shield followed by a 24-second cooldown.
4. Collect funding and venture opportunities. Opponents and arena activity are
   local bots; no account or network service is needed.
5. **Pause → Save & leave → Continue** restores the same company.
6. Marketplace purchases use earned game credits. Privacy, terms, support and
   deletion of local progress are in Settings.
